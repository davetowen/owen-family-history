    How to read the tree

    Parents and grandparents grow to the right. Children grow to the left. Brothers and sisters stack above and below.

    The coloured edge is the family line: the surname, or the maiden name for women.

    A dashed outline means the details are unconfirmed. ? means the family has an open question.

    Click anyone to centre the tree on them and see everything we know.

    Keys: → father, ⇧→ mother, ← child, ↑↓ brothers & sisters, / search.
